The Frank Anthony Public School offers four streams PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths), PCB (Physics,Chemistry, Biology), commerce, and arts.

Also the Frank Anthony Public School is a private, unaided, minority educational institution with campuses in several Indian cities, including New Delhi, Bengaluru, and Kolkata. Established by Frank Anthony, it is managed by the All-India Anglo-Indian Education Institution and is affiliated with the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE). The schools offer co-educational programs up to the senior secondary level and are known for their emphasis on extracurricular activities and a wide range of facilities.
